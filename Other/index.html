STATION GUIDE

STATION 01

The Herbivore Intake

Simulates selective herbivore feeding — only plant-based foods pass the biological filter.

✅ ACCEPTED FUEL
🥜 Nuts 🍃 Leaves 🍎 Berries 🥕 Carrot 🥬 Lettuce
❌ REJECTED ITEMS
🥩 Beef 🍗 Chicken 🐟 Fish 🥓 Pork
💡 Carnivore items will flash red and be rejected from the simulation. Herbivore items flash green and continue.
STATION 02

Vitamin Partition

Models the difference between fat-soluble and water-soluble vitamins and their absorption routes.

💧 WATER-SOLUBLE PATH

Flows right into a waste container — these vitamins cannot be stored and are excreted if not used immediately.

🧈 FAT-SOLUBLE PATH

Diverted left into a long-term storage tank — vitamins A, D, and K accumulate in fatty tissue.

💡 Watch how food items physically diverge to different sides after passing through the partition.
STATION 03

Nutritional Stress Test

Demonstrates the consequences of nutrient deficiency and insufficient energy on biological structures.

🦴 PHOSPHORUS PILLAR

A structural pillar labeled "Phosphorus" represents skeletal support. The system becomes unstable without it.

⚡ ENERGY GATE

The gate only opens when sufficient food volume is dropped. Below the threshold, the gate stays closed and blocks progress.

💡 Try adding many items at once versus just one to see the gate behavior change.
STATION 04

Pepsin pH Gate

Simulates the acidic stomach environment required for protein digestion via the enzyme Pepsin.

🧪 ACID TILT MECHANISM

A protein bead is only released when a high-acid tilt is triggered — mimicking the stomach's low pH (≈1.5–2).

🔬 NEUTRALIZATION ZONE

As the bead moves into the intestine section, the track levels out — representing pH neutralization by the pancreas, deactivating Pepsin.

💡 The enzyme only works in acidic zones. Observe how the bead's behavior changes as pH shifts.
STATION 05

Nutrient Divergence

Models the different absorption pathways of lipids versus carbohydrates and proteins in the small intestine.

🫙 LIPID ROUTE — LACTEAL/LYMPH

Fat particles are too large for direct capillary absorption. They enter the lacteal lymph pipe and bypass the portal vein system.

🩸 CARBS & PROTEINS — CAPILLARY

Smaller molecules like glucose and amino acids absorb directly into the capillary bloodstream via the portal vein.

💡 Each macronutrient takes a different route — neither path is "better," they serve different physiological purposes.
STATION 06

Microbiome Blockade

Represents the gut microbiome's role in the large intestine, particularly its effect on water reabsorption.

🛡️ FLORA INTACT STATE

With Bacterial Flora present, material moves slowly — water is reabsorbed properly as bacteria regulate transit time.

💊 ANTIBIOTIC STATE

Click ANTIBIOTICS to destroy the flora. Watch particles accelerate — water rushes through unabsorbed, simulating diarrhea.

💡 Drag Station 6 onto the playground, then use the buttons within it to toggle the bacterial flora state.
STATION 07

Comparative Adaptor

Demonstrates anatomical digestive differences between a seed-eating sparrow and a carnivorous hawk.

🐦 SPARROW SETUP

Includes a gizzard module filled with pebbles/sand for mechanical grinding of seeds. Features a longer digestive tract for plant matter.

🦅 HAWK SETUP

No gizzard required — meat is chemically digested. Features a shorter digestive tract optimized for high-protein processing.

💡 Digestive anatomy is an adaptation — form follows function based on dietary needs across species.
